OpenClaw Daily Reports

OpenClaw Daily Reports is a personal automation used to generate and archive development progress reports.

What the application does

The automation retrieves authorized Jira activity, summarizes development progress, and creates daily reports for the account owner.

Google Workspace integration

Google Drive and Google Docs are used to create, update, organize, and verify daily development reports in the owner's Google account.

Access and usage

This is a private personal automation. It does not offer public registration or accounts. Access to Google data requires the account owner's OAuth authorization.

Terms of use

This application is intended solely for the account owner's authorized personal reporting workflow.
