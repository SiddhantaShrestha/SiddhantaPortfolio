Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Purpose

OpenClaw Daily Reports is a personal automation for retrieving Jira development activity, generating progress reports, and archiving them in Google Drive.

2. Google data access

With the owner's authorization, the automation can access Google Drive and Google Docs to find, create, read, update, and verify reporting documents. The intended use is limited to managing the owner's development reports.

3. Data usage

Jira development information is used to prepare reports. Generated reports are archived in Google Drive and may be delivered to the owner's designated WhatsApp account.

4. Data storage and retention

Generated documents are retained in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. Google authorization credentials are managed locally by the authorized Google Workspace CLI.

5. Third party services

The automation uses Google Workspace, Atlassian Jira, WhatsApp delivery, and the AI processing service configured by the account owner. Data is used for the authorized reporting workflow and is not sold.

6. Access control

The automation is intended for personal use. No public user registration is provided. Google account access can be revoked through the owner's Google Account security settings.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site owner using the contact information provided on the portfolio website .
